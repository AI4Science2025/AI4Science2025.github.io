aiXBio Club

OmiX Hub (Bioinfo group) — a global community for AI×Bio and bioinformatics

OmiX Hub connects graduate students around the world to foster and enjoy discussions in AI×Bio and bioinformatics. The community runs a journal club, topic channels for machine learning and data science, member research talks, and conference meetups — all on Discord.

I serve as a co-host of the community. This page collects our upcoming events and updates; check back as new sessions are scheduled.

What Happens in the Community

Journal Club
Members present and discuss recent papers across AI×Bio and bioinformatics.
Machine Learning & Data Science Channels
Topic channels for methods, tooling, and hands-on questions — from model design to analysis pipelines.
Member Research Talks
Members share their own work and background with the group through short slide presentations.
Conference Meetups
Coordination around upcoming conferences — who is attending, what to see, and where to meet up.

Joining the Community

Two things are asked of everyone who joins:

  • Introduce yourself in the introduction channel — your name, where you are from, your background, what you do, and what you are interested in.
  • Use your real name (first and family name) as your server nickname. The group keeps things non-anonymous to build trust and a high-quality community.
